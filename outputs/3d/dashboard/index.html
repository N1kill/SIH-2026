CRITICAL EMERGENCY DIRECTIVE: MANDATORY EVACUATION
A catastrophic dam breach occurs at Machhu-II Dam (22.8212°N, 70.8414°E). A 6,647 m³/s flood peak propagates downstream into central Morbi within 3.2 to 7.5 hours, inundating street levels by up to 6.32 meters.

Key Emergency Parameters:

  • Total Exposed Population: 214,559 Persons (118,514 in Extreme Life-Threat Zone).
  • Total Flood Inundation Area: 123.38 km² along the Machhu river corridor.
  • Estimated Economic Loss: ₹3,629.14 Crores across industrial, housing, and agricultural sectors.
  • Critical Evacuation Priority Zone: 6.82 km² (CRI ≥ 75).
  • Evacuation Lead Window: 150 minutes from breach detection.

Evacuation Corridor Instructions:

  1. Primary Route (R1_EAST): Move all central Morbi residents EAST towards the East Bypass Ridge (>55m MSL) immediately. Avoid all Machhu river crossings.
  2. Secondary Route (R2_SOUTH): Peri-urban settlements south of Morbi must ascend to Vankaner elevated plateau.
  3. Designated Relief Hubs: Morbi East High Ground Shelter (25,000 capacity), South-East Camp (18,000 capacity), Liliya Ridge Hub (12,000 capacity).
